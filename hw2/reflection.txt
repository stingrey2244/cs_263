September 14th episode reflection

The episode first discussed ‘AI doomerism’; the hosts attended an AI data center conference , and it was noted that the biggest change to the industry within the past few years is  growing resistence on local/state/regional/national level. This resistance is only growing stronger, and warnings aren’t just coming from researchers, and regular citizens, but from the industry itself. Many are warning of an “AI doomsday”.

Despite this growing concern, nothing is slowing down; AI energy usage continues to rise, record energy consumption is ecpected in 2026/27, w/ data centers a major contributer. China/US account for 80% of projected increase in energy usage. It is predicted that power availability will become a major competive advantage in the coming years.
